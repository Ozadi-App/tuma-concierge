## 1. What this changes versus Fly Express

Fly Express is built around a **clock timetable** (a departure every 30 minutes, generated by `getAvailableTrips()`). **Peebee Taxipress is a live queue**, like a real taxi park rank. That affects every screen that showed times:

| Fly Express (timetable) | Peebee Taxipress (queue) |
|---|---|
| "Departures", "08:30 → 09:35", countdown | **"Vans in line"**: van 1 Boarding Now, van 2, van 3… |
| Search by date and time filter | Search by route and stage; "Book next van" or "Book a later van" |
| Fixed fare per corridor, fixed seats per departure | Fare per seat set by admin per route; seats come from the van the driver puts in the queue |
| Demo vans, drivers, plates generated from the clock | Real approved vans and drivers in the Partner app |
| "Leaves at 8:30" | "Leaves when it is full or the driver sets off" with an **estimated** time |

Passengers are booked onto **a specific van**, not a time slot. The seat map, luggage, return bundle, ticket and review screens carry over; the departure list and countdown are redesigned.

## 3. The queue model

### Van states (driver side)

```
Offline → In line (position n) → Boarding Now (position 1) → On Road → Arrived
              ↑                        │
              └── removed / dropped ───┘
```

- **Join the line** at the route's start stage (Kampala or Entebbe). Joins in order of arrival.
- **Boarding Now**: the driver at the front activates it. Only one van per route and stage is Boarding Now at a time.
- **On Road**: the driver taps it when the van is fully booked. The van leaves the line and everyone behind moves up one position.
- **Arrived**: ends the trip, settles money, opens ratings.

### Passenger side

- **Book next van**: the one Boarding Now, or the first in line with free seats.
- **Book a later van**: pick van 2, 3… in the line.
- **Pick-up along the route**: choose a pick-up point on the route; the driver sees an ordered pick-up list.
- A van's **seat count is live**; once booked seats equal capacity the van shows "Fully booked" and the driver is prompted to go On Road.

### Hard cases that need rules (all become admin settings)

1. A **booked van leaves the queue** (breakdown, driver goes offline). Passengers move to the next van, or are refunded.
2. A **later van moves up**, so a passenger's wait shrinks. Notify them ("Your van is now 2nd in line").
3. A **no-show** at boarding. Seat is released after N minutes; cash no-show fee.
4. **Walk-up passengers** at the stage who pay the driver directly and are not in the app. (Seat accounting must allow the driver to mark a seat taken.)
5. **Roadside pick-ups into free seats** on a van already On Road (see question 1).
6. **Queue-jumping and fairness**: a van should only be allowed to join the line when it is physically at the stage (location check, radius set in admin).
7. **Estimated departure/arrival** (the number the passenger sees) comes from recent average fill time per route, adjustable by admin.

## 5. Cash payments — how it maps onto Peebee

**[V] What already exists** (the boda/parcel "float" cash rail in `orders/routes.ts` ~L3011–3066):
- The customer pays the rider in cash. At settlement the platform commission (`fees.deliveryCommission` + surcharge + processing fee) is **subtracted from the rider's wallet**.
- It is **deliberately allowed to go negative**; the next digital payout nets against it automatically; withdrawals are blocked while in the red.
- An optional "deposit" mode (`isCashDepositOk`) blocks taking new jobs when the balance falls below a reserve floor.
- Fees and the cash-source mode are admin settings.

**What Taxipress needs that does not exist yet:**

1. **That code debits the `riders` table.** Taxipress drivers are Partner-app users whose earnings live on `users.wallet_balance` (Peebee car decision D2 `[V]`). A Taxipress settlement must debit the **users** wallet and allow it to go negative, with the same notification.
2. **Car rides do not use the cash rail today** `[V]` (no cash or float handling in `car/*.ts`). You said "like boda and car"; I am treating the boda rule as the model and will confirm.
3. **Settlement moment**: the commission is taken when the driver **confirms boarding / arrival**, not when the passenger books.
4. **Digital payments**: passenger pays by wallet or MoMo into escrow; on confirmed boarding the fare minus commission is **released to the driver's wallet automatically**. If the driver's balance is negative, it **nets first**, exactly as the boda rail does.
5. **Income record**: every seat, cash or digital, is posted to the ledger as driver income and platform commission, so reports and tax stay correct.
6. **Cash abuse controls** (admin settings, defaults conservative): maximum active cash bookings per passenger; no-show fee; block joining the queue below a reserve floor (existing deposit mode); a lower cash cap for new passengers.

Admin settings: Taxipress commission % (cash and digital, [?] same or different), cash on/off, deposit-floor mode, max cash bookings, no-show fee and minutes, cancellation retention %.

## 6. What cannot be copied as is

1. **Vanilla JS with HTML strings and inline styles (~486 KB).** Peebee is Next.js + React + Tailwind. This is a **rebuild from the design**, not an embed. Loading `index.html` in a frame would break Peebee's header, auth, wallet, theming and field rule.
2. **Colours.** Fly Express navy/red/blue is replaced by Peebee tokens (cream, ink, gold, `surface-*`). Layouts and flows carry over; palette does not.
3. **All data is fake**: vans, drivers, plates, OTP `123456`, wallet PIN `2580`, vouchers. None ships.
4. **Hard-coded rules** (18% VAT, UGX 500 seat fee, luggage prices, return price, retention %, cash hold, fuel %, driver allowance) become admin settings. Fly Express's corridor fares (5,000 / 7,000 / 20,000…) are **not** used; fares are set per route in admin.
5. **Brand and assets.** The logo is the Fly Express Travellers Association's; real driver and vehicle photos must be replaced.
6. **CDN scripts** (Leaflet, QR) → Peebee's bundled map and QR code.

## 7. How it would be built

- **Admin**: Taxipress settings and a **Routes** manager (add route by route): name, direction, start stage, ordered pick-up points, fare per seat, active toggle. First two routes: Kampala → Entebbe and Entebbe → Kampala.
- **API** (`apps/api/src/taxi/*`): routes, stages / pick-up points, queue entries (van, driver, route, position, status, timestamps), seat bookings, queue transitions with the rules in §3, taxi settlement (§5).
- **Bookings reuse the order engine.** Each booking carries a linked `orders` row so payments, chat, calls, tracking, ratings and refunds work unchanged `[V, PEEBEE_CAR_DESIGN D1]`.
- **Seat integrity**: a unique `(queue_entry_id, seat)` constraint plus a capacity check so two passengers cannot take the same seat; D1 has no row locks.
- **Real-time**: vans move up the line and seat counts change constantly. The customer app refreshes on a short poll plus push notifications, using Peebee's existing push.
- **Partner app**: new Taxipress queue screens (§4C) beside the existing jobs, vehicles and wallet screens. Vehicles need a **Taxipress category** (seats, layout) approved through the existing vehicle approval.
- **Migrations are applied by hand** `[V]`. Taxipress ships behind a flag **off by default** and uses `hasColumn()` guards. The PR will name the migration file to run.

## 10. Phase 0 build plan

### 10.1 Facts about the codebase that shape it `[V]`

- Latest migration is `0122_guest_checkout.sql`. The next is **`0123_taxipress.sql`**, applied by hand.
- `vehicle_categories.service_group` has a SQLite `CHECK (… 'standard','luxury','ambulance')`. Adding `taxi` would mean rebuilding that table on a live database. **I will not touch it.** Taxipress gets its own small tables and links to the existing `vehicles` rows.
- `car_driver_state.online` means "takes car ride requests". A driver in the Taxipress line must **not** also receive car jobs. Taxipress needs its own driver state, and going into the line pauses car matching.
- Driver earnings live on `users.wallet_balance`. The boda cash settlement debits `riders.*`. Taxipress needs its own settlement on the users wallet.
- The customer app already has `CodeQr`, `LiveTrackingMap`, `PlaceSearch`, `PassengerCard`, and `HoldToCall`; the Partner app has `QrScanner` and `QrCodeImage`. Boarding confirmation reuses these.
- `hasColumn()` guards columns; new tables need a `hasTable()` guard (already used for `car_withdrawals`). The whole feature returns "not available" until the migration is applied.

### 10.3 API (`apps/api/src/taxipress/`)

| Area | Endpoints (all under `/v1/taxipress`) |
|---|---|
| Public config | `GET /config` (on/off, routes with fares, stage names, seat fee, cash allowed) |

Transitions are guarded in one place (like `carpool.ts` does) so a van cannot skip states. Removing a van from the line **moves its bookings to the next van that has space, or refunds them**, and notifies every passenger.

### 10.4 Money flow

1. **Digital**: passenger pays wallet or MoMo into escrow at booking. When the driver confirms boarding, the fare **minus commission** is released to the driver's wallet. If their balance is negative, it **nets first**, the same as the boda rail.
2. **Cash**: the seat is booked unpaid. When the driver confirms boarding and **marks cash received**, the fare is recorded as driver income and the **commission is debited from the driver's wallet**, allowed to go negative, with a push notification.
3. **Block rule**: if enabled, `POST /driver/join` refuses when the balance is below the floor, with a clear "top up to continue" message.
4. **Commission** is computed from the settings in §9 at settlement, stored on the booking, and posted to the ledger as platform income.
5. Refunds and no-show fees follow the cancellation and no-show settings.

### 10.7 Risks to settle during the build

1. **Seat race conditions**: handled by the unique `(queue_id, seat_label)` constraint and a capacity check inside one transaction.

## 12. Relay delivery connection: one taxi, one approval, two switches

### 12.1 How the relay works today `[V]`

Read from `apps/api/src/relay/` and migrations `0102`, `0106`, `0107`.

- A long parcel is split into **3 legs**: **leg 1** boda → boarding stage, **leg 2 matatu** → drop stage or petrol station, **leg 3** boda → customer (`order_relay_legs`).
- **Routes** are `relay_routes` (stage A ⇄ stage B, a `parcel_fare`, short codes like `KLA ⇄ EBB`). **Stops** between are `relay_route_stages` (ordered, may be Peebee Gas stations). Both are managed in Admin → Settings → Relay.
- **Matatu operators** are `relay_operators` (user, **one route**, plate, `pending → approved → suspended`). They apply in the Partner app's relay page and staff approve them. **Today they only carry parcels.**
- **Handoffs use codes.** Leg 1's rider gives a code to the operator (`receiveAtMatatu`); the operator hands the parcel to the customer or last rider with another code. A parcel may only be received by an approved operator **on that route**.
- The operator shares the matatu's position, and customers get "arriving in N minutes", "the matatu is at your stage" and "missed it → petrol station" notices.
- **Pay**: the matatu leg's share goes to the operator's wallet (`creditWallet`, `users.wallet_balance`); a negative balance nets against it automatically.

### 12.2 The matatu and the Taxipress taxi are the same vehicle

A Ugandan matatu, a taxi, a minibus and a Taxipress vehicle are one thing. So **there is no "relay matatu" fleet beside a "Taxipress" fleet.** There is one list of approved taxis, and each taxi can do two jobs:

| Switch | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Passengers** | The taxi can join the Taxipress line and take seat bookings |
| **Parcels** | The taxi can be the middle leg of a relay delivery |

| Passengers | Parcels | What the taxi is |
|---|---|---|
| On | On | Normal Taxipress taxi that also carries relay parcels (the expected case) |
| On | Off | Passengers only |
| Off | On | Parcels only, for example on a route where Taxipress is not live yet, or an existing relay operator |
| Off | Off | Not working (suspended) |

How the switches are set:

1. **One approval per taxi** in Admin: driver, plate, route, 14 or 18 seats. Peebee sets which of the two switches the taxi is **allowed** (defaults in §12.5).
2. **The driver chooses day to day.** Being in the passenger line is the driver tapping **Join the line** (Passengers), and a driver can pause **Accept parcels**. A driver can turn a switch *off* but never *on* beyond what Peebee approved.
3. **Existing relay operators** are carried over as **Parcels on, Passengers off**. Nobody applies twice and nothing stops working. To also take passengers, they add their seat layout and Peebee switches Passengers on.
4. A Taxipress driver can no longer apply separately as a relay operator. The existing relay application page is replaced by the single taxi approval.

### 12.3 How the two systems connect

1. **One route and stage registry.** A Taxipress route **is** a relay route plus a direction (`taxipress_routes.relay_route_id`). **Kampala Stage** and **Entebbe Stage** are stage A and stage B of one `relay_routes` row ("KLA ⇄ EBB"). Passenger pick-up points are the existing `relay_route_stages`. A rename in Admin changes tickets, queue and parcels at once.
2. **One approval, mirrored into the relay.** Approving a taxi with Parcels on creates or updates the driver's `relay_operators` row (source `taxipress`, status approved, same route and plate). Switching Parcels off suspends that row.
3. **A parcel attaches to the taxi that takes it.**
   - A taxi with **Passengers on** may receive a parcel only when it is **Boarding Now** (admin can allow **any taxi in line**) **at that boarding stage, heading the direction the parcel travels** (the relay already computes `KLA - EBB` or `EBB - KLA`). Leg 2 then records `taxipress_queue_id`.
   - A **Parcels-only** taxi works as today: it receives parcels by code on its route.
4. **Parcels are cargo, not seats.** They never count toward "fully booked". Admin sets a **maximum parcels per trip**; at the limit the taxi stops accepting more.
5. **One driver screen.** The Taxipress manifest in the Partner app lists **passengers** (seat, pick-up stop, paid or cash due) and **parcels** (receive by code, drop stop, hand to customer by code, customer missed it), ordered by stop. The existing relay page becomes a tab of the same screen, and Parcels-only drivers still see it on its own.
6. **Automatic sync.**
   - The taxi's location pings feed `shareMatatuPosition` for **every parcel on board**, so there is no separate "share position" action and the customer gets true "arriving in N minutes".
   - **Reached stop** marks the parcels dropping there as arrived and sends the existing notices.
   - **On Road** starts leg 2 for the parcels received; **Arrived** closes the trip and flags any parcel still on board.
7. **Mid-route drops.** A parcel's drop point can be any stop (or a petrol station that is a stop). The taxi's stop list comes from the same `relay_route_stages` as passenger pick-ups.
8. **Money.** The parcel leg pays through the existing relay payout into the driver's wallet, so **negative cash-commission balances net against it automatically**. Relay parcels are already **prepaid in the app**, so no cash rule is needed. Seat commission settings (§9) do not apply to the parcel leg; the relay's own fee and per-leg release settings do. Reports show **seat income** and **parcel income** separately.
9. **Customer experience.** The relay quote and order screens show the **real next taxi** ("Boarding now: UBM 245K", "2nd in line, about 20 min") taken from the queue. The first rider is told which taxi to hand the parcel to.
10. **Sandbox.** The relay's sandbox demo roles gain a **demo Taxipress taxi** so testers see the whole parcel-in-taxi journey.

### 12.6 When a parcel cannot go on: the office network

**Why it happens.** With "Boarding Now only", the rider can arrive when the taxis in line are not yet boarding, when none is in line, or when the boarding taxi is at its parcel limit. The rider must not sit there for an hour. The same landing place also catches every other failed delivery (see "bounced orders").

**The flow**

1. The rider taps **Arrived at the stage** (location check). Peebee looks for an eligible taxi. If there is one, the normal code handoff happens.
2. If none: the parcel is marked **Awaiting a taxi**. The rider sees a countdown and the options admin has switched on. The customer is told ("Your parcel is waiting for the next taxi").
3. The rider waits up to **N minutes** (admin). Waiting time can be paid through the existing waiting-fee mechanism (`orders/time-fees.ts`) if admin turns that on. If a taxi becomes eligible, the handoff happens.
4. After N minutes, or sooner if the rider taps it, the rider chooses from the options admin enabled:

   | Option | Default |
   |---|---|
   | **Keep waiting** (admin can cap the total wait) | Available |
   | **Take it to a Peebee office** | **Default option** |
   | **Take it back to the shop / sender** | **Off.** Admin can switch it on |

   Admin can also let Peebee decide for the rider instead of offering a choice.

5. **The rider is always paid by Peebee** for the extra distance, immediately, never waiting for the customer. Who bears the cost is a policy, described in the advice box below.

**Offices are a network**

- **Several offices.** The existing **Entebbe office** is the first one. Offices in **Kampala** (some opened by other people) follow, each one added in Admin.
- **Admin registers an office** in the Admin app: name, address, **map location**, opening hours, on/off switch, and whether it is run by **Peebee** or by a **partner**.
- **Admin assigns one or more staff accounts to each office.** Those accounts use an **Office desk** page inside the Admin app, restricted so a staff member sees **only their own office(s)**. They can receive a parcel by code, release it, and resend it. They cannot open the rest of Admin. A new permission governs this.
- **Which office to use.** The rider is shown the **nearest open office** to the stage where they are, with distance and hours. Admin can set a preferred office per stage. Closed or full offices are hidden.
- **Any office receives any bounced order, regardless of which office it is.** An office does not need to be linked to the route or the stage. (The stage link only decides which office is suggested first.)
- **Handling fee.** A partner office can earn a fee per parcel held. Admin sets whether there is one, the amount (flat or per day), and who bears it.
- **Accountability.** Every receive and release is logged with the person, time and code. Loss and damage rules are open (§12.11).

**Bounced orders and "needs resending"**

An order **bounces** when it cannot be delivered for any reason. Each bounce has a **cause**, which decides who pays:

| Cause | Examples | Whose side |
|---|---|---|
| **No taxi available** | none boarding, parcel limit reached | Peebee |
| **Driver refused the parcel** | does not fit, not allowed by the rules | Customer (parcel did not meet the rules) |
| **Customer could not be reached** | wrong address, phone off, missed the meeting at the stage | Customer |
| **Customer refused** | refused at the door | Customer |

When the parcel reaches any office, the staff member enters its code. The order then shows **"Needs resending"** with its cause. Staff choose how to get it to the customer **by any means available in the app**:

- **Send again through the relay**, once a taxi is Boarding Now (a new first leg office → boarding stage, a new rider, then the normal taxi and last leg).
- **Send directly by boda** (one rider, office → customer).
- **Customer collects at the office** (shows their code).
- **Send to a different address** the customer gives.

The customer is notified at each step and can also **Send again** or **Cancel** from the order page. The cost of each option follows the policy below.

**Peebee's advice on charging and time limits** (you asked how Amazon handles this)

*What Amazon does, as far as its public pages show.* Amazon's help page says that a package returned as undeliverable **cannot be reshipped** and the customer places a new order, and that the **refund follows after the return is received** (about 5 to 7 business days, by region). Third-party sources put delivery attempts at about **three**, locker pickup at about **three days**, and counter pickup at about **14 days** (these come from blogs and press material, not a single official page, so check before copying numbers). The pattern is: **the customer is not billed for a logistics failure, the hold time is limited and reminders are sent, and an uncollected parcel goes back and is refunded automatically.**

*My recommendation for Peebee:*

| Topic | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Who pays the detour** | **Depends on the cause.** Peebee pays when **Peebee caused it** (no taxi available). The **customer pays** when the **customer caused it** (unreachable, refused, parcel not allowed). | A customer should not pay for a failure they did not cause. A customer who causes repeated failures should. This is Amazon's logic: logistics failures are the company's cost. |
| **When to charge the customer** | **Not at the moment it strands.** Charge only when the parcel is sent again, and only for customer-caused causes. | It avoids charging for a problem that may resolve itself, and it keeps the customer happy while the parcel is waiting. |
| **If the customer will not pay** | The parcel stays at the office until the hold time ends, then it is cancelled and refunded **minus** the customer-caused detour. | A clear end point. |
| **Hold time at the office** | **7 days** by default, with reminders at day 1, 3 and 6. **Perishable orders**: 24 to 48 hours. | Amazon's windows range from about three to 14 days. Seven is a balanced starting value for a small office. |
| **Automatic cancel and refund** | At the end of the hold time the order is cancelled and refunded to the customer's wallet. Store orders go back to the shop, per the shop's agreement. | Same as Amazon's "returned, then refunded". |
| **Resend attempts** | **Up to 2 resends** per order, then cancel and refund. | Amazon's roughly three attempts. |
| **Detour rider pay** | **Reuse the existing delivery per-km rate** (and minimum fare). Admin can add a separate stranded-parcel rate later if needed. | Nothing new to set up or explain. |

All of these are **admin settings with these as the defaults**. You can look at them and change them.

**Data and code impact**
- New tables `relay_holding_points`, `relay_holding_staff`, `relay_holds`, `relay_extra_legs` (§10.2). Extra legs are kept outside `order_relay_legs`, because that table only allows legs 1 to 3 and a `kind` of `boda` or `matatu` (a `CHECK` constraint). Changing it would mean rebuilding a live table. I will not do that.
- The rider's job list already shows open relay legs; extra legs are added to it.
- The re-dispatch rider must be set as the order's rider for the stage handoff to work (`receiveAtMatatu` checks `orders.rider_id`). The exact mechanism is to be verified in the build `[A]`.
- Everything stays behind `hasTable()` / `hasColumn()`, so the existing relay keeps working until `0123` is applied.

### 12.7 Parcel commission: two modes, one active at a time

| Mode | How the taxi driver's parcel income is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Existing fee split** (default) | The matatu leg gets its share of the order's fee pool, exactly as today |
| **Taxipress parcel commission** | Peebee takes an extra commission from the matatu leg's income: a **percentage** or a **flat amount** (admin chooses) |

- Setting `taxipress_parcel_commission_mode`: `relay_split` or `taxipress_extra`, plus percent or flat value.
- Every change is written to the activity log with who and when, so you can see which mode was active at any time. If you want a mode to switch on a given date automatically, say so and I'll add an "effective from" date.
- The commission is stored on each parcel leg at payout, so past parcels never change when the setting changes.

### 12.8 Parcel rules: simple by design

**Principle: a parcel is a parcel. If it fits, it goes.** No size classes, no weights, no categories to pick. Business cargo is allowed if it fits.

What admin controls:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| **Maximum parcels per taxi trip**, separately for **14-seaters** and **18-seaters** | Admin enters |
| **"Does it fit?" guide**: one short line shown to customers and drivers (for example "Must fit in the taxi's luggage space and be carried by one person") and optionally a **largest dimension** | Admin enters |
| **Not-allowed list**: a short admin-edited list of goods taxis will not carry (for example charcoal, fuel, wet or raw food). Light food is fine | Admin enters |
| **Customer confirmation** when ordering: "My parcel follows the rules" | On |

How it works:
- **Count, not weight.** Each relay order counts as **one parcel**. A multi-store bag that already travels as one bag counts as one. The taxi's manifest shows **parcels carried / parcels allowed**.
- **The driver has the final say** at the handoff. If the parcel does not fit or is not allowed, the driver can refuse it, and it follows the bounced-order flow with the cause "Driver refused the parcel".
- The relay quote shows the rule line before the customer pays. Nothing else to fill in.
- Because nothing is captured about size or weight, **no new fields are needed on the parcel order** except the confirmation tick.

### 12.10 Risks

1. **Offices are physical operations, some run by other people.** Staff, opening hours, storage, and responsibility for loss. Partner offices need vetting, an agreement, and possibly a deposit.
2. **Cause disputes.** "Customer unreachable" can be disputed. Staff need a reason field and the app should record the rider's call attempts and location.
3. **Perishable and store orders** may not survive a delay. Admin can exclude them from relay or set a short hold.

6. **Migrating existing relay operators** needs a one-off script; operators without a plate stay Parcels-only.
7. **A taxi removed from the line after taking a parcel**: staff move the parcel to another taxi from the Admin queue monitor (new action).
